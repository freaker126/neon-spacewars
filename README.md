# 🛸 Freaker126 Neon Space Wars

A single-file retro neon space shooter built with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript! Designed for frictionless browser play across mobile devices and desktop computers with zero external downloads or asset dependencies.

👉 [**Play Freaker126 Neon Space Wars Live Here**](https://freaker126.github.io/neon-spacewars/)

## 🎮 How to Play & Controls

### 📱 Mobile Phone / Tablet (Portrait Recommended)

* **Right Thumb (Floating Dynamic Joypad):** Touch anywhere on the right half of the screen to spawn a floating joystick directly under your thumb. Drag in any direction to move your spaceship.

* **Left Thumb (Action Buttons):** 
  * Tap **FIRE** (bottom-left) to launch primary lasers.
  * Tap **BOMB** (beside FIRE) to clear screen projectiles and deal massive area damage.

### 💻 Computer / Keyboard

* **Movement:** Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** to steer your ship.

* **Fire:** Hold **K** or **Spacebar** to fire your primary laser cannon.

* **Smart Bomb:** Press **B** to detonate a bomb.

## ✨ Features

* 🚀 **5-Tier Weapon Upgrades:** Collect **`W`** loot drops from destroyed aliens to upgrade from a basic cyan laser to dual green plasma, triple pink spread, yellow quad blasters, and ultra-wide purple photon sweeps.

* 💣 **Smart Bombs:** Collect **`B`** loot drops to store bombs that instantly vaporize all normal enemies and deal heavy damage to bosses.

* 👾 **Varied Alien Forces:** Fight geometry-based alien invaders including fast stars, tanky squares, rectangle cruisers, and aggressive red kamikaze ships that track your position.

* 🛸 **1-on-1 Boss Battles:** Clear wave quotas to trigger **"BOSS INCOMING!"** warnings and duel massive hexagon motherships with scaling health and bullet-hell attack patterns.

* 🎵 **Synthesized Web Audio:** Procedurally generated synth music, laser blasts, explosions, and powerup audio powered by the native browser Web Audio API.

* 🏆 **Local High Scores:** Persistence system saves your best scores directly in browser `localStorage`.

## 🛠️ Offline Play

Because this entire arcade game is self-contained inside a single `index.html` file, you can download the code, double-click the file on any machine, and play offline in Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge with zero internet connection required!
